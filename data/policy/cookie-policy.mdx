## Introduction

Cookies are small text files that may be placed on your device when you interact with online services. They help websites remember details about your visit, enhance your experience on subsequent visits, understand service usage, and support analytics efforts. Cookies set directly by us are called first-party cookies. We also use third-party cookies, which originate from a domain different from the one you are currently visiting. Similar technologies, such as pixels, web beacons, sharing of device IDs and other identifiers via APIs or local storage, can also be used for these purposes. For simplicity, we also refer to these technologies as “cookies” in this Cookie Policy.
